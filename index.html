    Statics of Rigid Bodies

    ENGMEC1 · Three (3) units · Prerequisites: ENGPHYS (H) / CALENG2 (S)

    ENGMEC1 is a branch of Engineering Mechanics that deals with the study of forces and their effects on rigid bodies in static equilibrium. This course involves the analysis of two- and three-dimensional structures to determine external and internal effects resulting from applied forces. Key geometric properties such as centroids and moments of inertia, critical for structural design, are also covered — forming the foundation for further study in the mechanics of deformable bodies, structural analysis, and design.

    Course learning outcomes (CLO)

    On completion of the course, the student is expected to do the following:

    CLO 1

    Identify and determine the components and resultant of forces and force systems in 2D and 3D.

    CLO 2

    Analyze the effects of forces on rigid bodies in static equilibrium using free body diagrams and equations of equilibrium.

    CLO 3

    Analyze the external and internal effects of forces on structures such as beams, trusses, frames, and simple machines.

    CLO 4

    Analyze the effects of friction forces on rigid bodies in static equilibrium.

    CLO 5

    Solve the properties (centroid, center of gravity, and moment of inertia) of areas and lines, and apply these properties in equilibrium problems.
